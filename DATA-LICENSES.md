# Data licenses

The Terrane code and the icons as distributed are released under the MIT license (see `LICENSE`). The shapes are
derived from the geographic datasets below. None of them is share-alike, and none of them comes from OpenStreetMap.
Where a license asks for attribution, this file is that attribution.

## Relief

- Natural Earth Cross Blended Hypsometric Tints with Shaded Relief (`HYP_HR_SR_OB_DR`), public domain.
- Terrain Tiles on AWS (Mapzen / Tilezen joerd), which compile SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1, NED, EU-DEM, CDEM, and other
  elevation sources. Their attribution requirements are listed at
  [github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md).

## Symbols

The `simbolos` pictograms are original drawings made for Terrane and are covered by the MIT license with the rest of
the package.
